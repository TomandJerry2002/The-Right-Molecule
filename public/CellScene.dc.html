Pancreas

Makes insulin, the hormone that lets cells take in glucose from the blood. When cells respond less well to insulin, the pancreas makes more of it. It also releases enzymes that help digest food.

Releases eggs and makes reproductive hormones, including small amounts of androgens. In some women, high insulin can increase how much androgen the ovaries make.
